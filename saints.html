JANUARY

1 Jan - Mary, Mother of God

Catholic: We begin the year under Mary's mantle, Mother of God.

"Behold, your mother." - John 19:27

Latin: Mater Dei = Mother of God

Prayer: Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us. Amen.

3 Jan - Holy Name of Jesus

Catholic: At the Name of Jesus every knee shall bow.

"There is no other name under heaven given to men by which we must be saved." - Acts 4:12

Latin: Iesus = God Saves

Prayer: Jesus, we adore Your Holy Name. Amen.

6 Jan - Epiphany

Catholic: The Magi adored Christ. We adore Him in the Eucharist.

"They fell down and worshiped him." - Matthew 2:11

Latin: Epiphania = Manifestation

Prayer: Lord, we bring You our hearts. Amen.

17 Jan - St. Anthony the Abbot

Catholic: Father of monks, man of prayer in the desert.

"Pray without ceasing." - 1 Thessalonians 5:17

Latin: Oratio = Prayer

Prayer: St. Anthony, teach us to pray. Amen.

21 Jan - St. Agnes

Catholic: Virgin Martyr at 13, pure for Christ.

"Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God." - Matthew 5:8

Latin: Agnus = Lamb

Prayer: St. Agnes, pray for our youth. Amen.

28 Jan - St. Thomas Aquinas

Catholic: Doctor of the Church, faith and reason.

"You shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free." - John 8:32

Latin: Veritas = Truth

Prayer: St. Thomas, help us seek truth. Amen.

FEBRUARY

2 Feb - Presentation of the Lord

Catholic: Mary and Joseph present Jesus in the Temple.

"A light for revelation to the Gentiles." - Luke 2:32

Latin: Lumen = Light

Prayer: Jesus, Light of the world, enlighten us. Amen.

11 Feb - Our Lady of Lourdes

Catholic: Mary calls us to penance and Rosary.

"Do whatever he tells you." - John 2:5

Latin: Immaculata = Spotless

Prayer: Our Lady of Lourdes, pray for the sick. Amen.

14 Feb - St. Valentine

Catholic: True love is sacrifice as Christ loved.

"Greater love has no man than this, that a man lay down his life for his friends." - John 15:13

Latin: Caritas = True Love

Prayer: St. Valentine, teach us to love. Amen.

22 Feb - Chair of St. Peter

Catholic: Authority Christ gave to Peter and the Popes.

"You are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church." - Matthew 16:18

Latin: Cathedra = Chair

Prayer: St. Peter, pray for the Church. Amen.

MARCH

17 Mar - St. Patrick

Catholic: Taught the Trinity with the Shamrock.

"Go therefore and make disciples of all nations." - Matthew 28:19

Latin: Trinitas = Trinity

Prayer: St. Patrick, strengthen our faith. Amen.

19 Mar - St. Joseph

Catholic: Protector of Holy Family and Church.

"Joseph, son of David, do not fear to take Mary your wife." - Matthew 1:20

Latin: Custos = Guardian

Prayer: St. Joseph, protect our families. Amen.

25 Mar - Annunciation

Catholic: The Word became Flesh. We pray the Angelus.

"Behold, I am the handmaid of the Lord; let it be to me according to your word." - Luke 1:38

Latin: Fiat = Let it be done

Prayer: Mary, help us say Fiat. Amen.

APRIL

Easter - Resurrection

Catholic: Christ is Risen! Centre of our faith.

"He is not here; he has risen." - Luke 24:6

Latin: Resurrectio = Resurrection

Prayer: Risen Jesus, fill us with joy. Amen.

Divine Mercy Sunday

Catholic: Jesus, I trust in You.

"Blessed are the merciful, for they shall obtain mercy." - Matthew 5:7

Latin: Misericordia = Mercy

Prayer: Jesus, I trust in You. Amen.

25 Apr - St. Mark

Catholic: Proclaimed the Gospel to the world.

"Go into all the world and preach the gospel." - Mark 16:15

Latin: Evangelium = Good News

Prayer: St. Mark, help us proclaim the Gospel. Amen.

29 Apr - St. Catherine of Siena

Catholic: Loved the Church and the Pope.

"That they may all be one." - John 17:21

Latin: Ecclesia = Church

Prayer: St. Catherine, pray for unity. Amen.

MAY

1 May - St. Joseph the Worker

Catholic: Work offered to God becomes prayer.

"Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord." - Colossians 3:23

Latin: Labor = Work

Prayer: St. Joseph, bless our work. Amen.

13 May - Our Lady of Fatima

Catholic: Pray the Rosary daily for peace.

"Pray always and do not lose heart." - Luke 18:1

Latin: Rosarium = Rosary

Prayer: Our Lady of Fatima, pray for us. Amen.

22 May - St. Rita

Catholic: Patron of impossible cases.

"With God all things are possible." - Matthew 19:26

Latin: Patientia = Patience

Prayer: St. Rita, pray for our needs. Amen.

31 May - Visitation

Catholic: Mary brings Jesus to Elizabeth.

"My soul magnifies the Lord." - Luke 1:46

Latin: Magnificat = Glorifies

Prayer: Mary, help us bring Jesus to all. Amen.

JUNE

13 Jun - St. Anthony of Padua

Catholic: Held the Child Jesus, patron of lost things.

"Ask, and it will be given you; seek, and you will find." - Matthew 7:7

Latin: Fides = Faith

Prayer: St. Anthony, pray for us. Amen.

Sacred Heart of Jesus

Catholic: His Heart burns with love in the Blessed Sacrament.

"Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest." - Matthew 11:28

Latin: Cor Iesu = Heart of Jesus

Prayer: Sacred Heart of Jesus, have mercy. Amen.

29 Jun - Sts. Peter & Paul

Catholic: Two pillars of the Church.

"You are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church." - Matthew 16:18

Latin: Petra = Rock

Prayer: Sts. Peter and Paul, pray for us. Amen.

JULY

11 Jul - St. Benedict

Catholic: Ora et Labora - Pray and Work.

"Pray constantly." - 1 Thessalonians 5:17

Latin: Ora et Labora = Pray and Work

Prayer: St. Benedict, teach us to pray. Amen.

16 Jul - Our Lady of Mount Carmel

Catholic: Mary of the Brown Scapular.

"Behold your mother." - John 19:27

Latin: Scapulare = Scapular

Prayer: Our Lady of Carmel, protect us. Amen.

31 Jul - St. Ignatius of Loyola

Catholic: For the Greater Glory of God.

"Do all to the glory of God." - 1 Corinthians 10:31

Latin: Ad Maiorem Dei Gloriam

Prayer: St. Ignatius, teach us. Amen.

AUGUST

4 Aug - St. John Vianney

Catholic: Patron of priests, man of Confession.

"Whose sins you forgive are forgiven them." - John 20:23

Latin: Confessio = Confession

Prayer: St. John Vianney, pray for priests. Amen.

15 Aug - Assumption of Mary

Catholic: Taken body and soul into Heaven.

"The Mighty One has done great things for me." - Luke 1:49

Latin: Assumptio = Taken Up

Prayer: Our Lady, pray for us. Amen.

28 Aug - St. Augustine

Catholic: From sinner to Saint by grace.

"You have made us for yourself, O Lord, and our heart is restless until it rests in you." - St. Augustine, Confessions

Latin: Gratia = Grace

Prayer: St. Augustine, pray for conversion. Amen.

SEPTEMBER

8 Sep - Nativity of Mary

Catholic: Dawn of our salvation.

"Blessed are you among women." - Luke 1:42

Latin: Nativitas = Birth

Prayer: Holy Mary, we celebrate you. Amen.

14 Sep - Holy Cross

Catholic: By Your Holy Cross You redeemed the world.

"God shows his love for us in that while we were yet sinners Christ died for us." - Romans 5:8

Latin: Crux = Cross

Prayer: We adore You, O Christ. Amen.

23 Sep - St. Padre Pio

Catholic: Pray, hope, don't worry.

"Cast all your anxieties on him, for he cares about you." - 1 Peter 5:7

Latin: Stigmata = Wounds

Prayer: Padre Pio, teach us to trust. Amen.

29 Sep - Archangels Michael, Gabriel, Raphael

Catholic: Protect us in battle.

"He will give his angels charge of you." - Psalm 91:11

Latin: Angelus = Messenger

Prayer: St. Michael, defend us. Amen.

OCTOBER

1 Oct - St. Therese of Lisieux

Catholic: Little Way - small things with great love.

"Unless you become like children, you will never enter the kingdom of heaven." - Matthew 18:3

Latin: Parva Via = Little Way

Prayer: St. Therese, teach us. Amen.

4 Oct - St. Francis of Assisi

Catholic: Instrument of peace.

"Blessed are the peacemakers." - Matthew 5:9

Latin: Pax et Bonum = Peace and Good

Prayer: Make us instruments of peace. Amen.

7 Oct - Our Lady of the Rosary

Catholic: Meditate on Christ with Mary.

"His mother kept all these things in her heart." - Luke 2:51

Latin: Rosarium = Rosary

Prayer: Our Lady of the Rosary, pray for us. Amen.

22 Oct - St. John Paul II

Catholic: Do not be afraid! Open doors to Christ.

"Be not afraid." - John 6:20

Latin: Totus Tuus = Totally Yours

Prayer: St. John Paul II, pray for youth. Amen.

1 Nov - All Saints

Catholic: Our goal is Heaven.

"Be holy, for I am holy." - 1 Peter 1:16

Latin: Sanctus = Holy

Prayer: All Saints, pray for us. Amen.

2 Nov - All Souls

Catholic: We pray for souls in Purgatory.

"Eternal rest grant unto them, O Lord." - 2 Maccabees 12:46

Latin: Requiescat in Pace = Rest in Peace

Prayer: Eternal rest grant unto them. Amen.

21 Nov - Presentation of Mary

Catholic: Mary gave her whole life to God.

"Behold, I am the handmaid of the Lord." - Luke 1:38

Latin: Praesentatio = Presentation

Prayer: Holy Mary, help us. Amen.

22 Nov - St. Cecilia

Catholic: Sang to God even in martyrdom.

"Sing to the Lord a new song." - Psalm 96:1

Latin: Cantate Domino = Sing to the Lord

Prayer: St. Cecilia, help us sing God's praise. Amen.

30 Nov - St. Andrew

Catholic: Brought others to Christ.

"Come, follow me, and I will make you fishers of men." - Matthew 4:19

Latin: Piscator Hominum = Fisher of Men

Prayer: St. Andrew, help us bring others. Amen.

Catholic: Conceived without sin to be Mother of Jesus.

"Hail, full of grace, the Lord is with you." - Luke 1:28

Latin: Immaculata = Spotless

Prayer: O Mary conceived without sin, pray for us. Amen.

12 Dec - Our Lady of Guadalupe

Catholic: Mother of the Americas.

"Blessed is she who believed." - Luke 1:45

Latin: Mater Nostra = Our Mother

Prayer: Our Lady of Guadalupe, pray for us. Amen.

13 Dec - St. Lucy

Catholic: Her name means Light.

"You are the light of the world." - Matthew 5:14

Latin: Lux = Light

Prayer: St. Lucy, help us see by faith. Amen.

25 Dec - Christmas

Catholic: The Word became Flesh.

"And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us." - John 1:14

Latin: Natus Est = He is Born

Prayer: Infant Jesus, we adore You. Amen.
