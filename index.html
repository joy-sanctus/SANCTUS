📖 TODAY - 21 Nov - Presentation of Mary

The Presentation of the Blessed Virgin Mary

Who was she? When Mary was a little girl, her parents Joachim and Anne took her to the Temple in Jerusalem. They presented her to God, to say: "Lord, this child belongs to You."

Why we celebrate her: Mary gave her whole life to God from childhood. She shows us how to say YES to God early, with a pure heart.

Feast Day: 21 November

🙏 Prayer: Holy Mary, presented in the Temple, help us to present our lives to God every day. Teach us to say Yes like you did. Amen.

📚 Latin Word: Praesentatio = Presentation
Say it: pray-zen-TAH-tee-oh. Example: Praesentatio Mariae - The Presentation of Mary.
